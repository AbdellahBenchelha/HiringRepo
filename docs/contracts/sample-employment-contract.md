# Sample Employment Contract

> SPECIMEN DOCUMENT - THIS IS NOT A JOB OFFER AND NOT A BINDING AGREEMENT.
> It is provided so that you can read our standard employment terms before you
> decide whether to accept an offer. Nothing in this document creates any
> obligation for you or for the Company.

## How to read this document

Every item written in [SQUARE BRACKETS] is a placeholder. In a real contract
those placeholders are replaced with the terms that apply to you personally -
your job title, your salary, your start date, your working hours and your place
of work.

Where this specimen and your written offer letter differ, **your offer letter
and the final signed contract are the documents that count.** This specimen has
no legal effect.

The final contract you receive may also differ from this specimen depending on
the role, the site you work from, the country of employment and any collective
agreement that applies there. If anything here is unclear, ask us before you
accept - questions are welcome and they will not affect your application.

## 1. The parties

This agreement is made between:

- **The Company:** [REGISTERED COMPANY NAME], registered at [REGISTERED ADDRESS],
  registration number [REGISTRATION NUMBER] (referred to below as "the Company").
- **The Employee:** [FULL NAME], residing at [EMPLOYEE ADDRESS] (referred to
  below as "the Employee").

## 2. Position

The Employee is engaged as **[JOB TITLE]**, reporting to [MANAGER OR ROLE].

A description of the main duties of the position is attached to the contract.
The Company may reasonably adjust those duties over time to fit the needs of the
business, the Employee's skills and any training completed, provided the changes
remain consistent with the position.

## 3. Place of work

The usual place of work is [WORK LOCATION / SITE ADDRESS].

The work arrangement for this position is [ON-SITE / HYBRID / REMOTE]. Where the
arrangement is hybrid or remote, the Company will confirm in writing how many
days are worked on site, what equipment is provided and what the Employee is
expected to provide (for example a suitable internet connection and a quiet
workspace).

## 4. Start date and duration

Employment begins on [START DATE].

The contract is [PERMANENT / FIXED-TERM]. Where it is fixed-term, it ends on
[END DATE] unless it is renewed or extended in writing.

## 5. Probationary period

The probationary period is [LENGTH OF PROBATION].

During this period either party may end the contract with [PROBATION NOTICE
PERIOD] notice in writing. The Company may extend the probationary period once,
in writing and before it ends, where local law allows this.

## 6. Working hours

Normal working time is [NUMBER] hours per week, worked over [NUMBER] days.

This role is scheduled in shifts, which may include [EVENINGS / WEEKENDS /
PUBLIC HOLIDAYS / NIGHT SHIFTS]. Schedules are published at least [NOTICE
PERIOD FOR SCHEDULES] in advance. Rest breaks and daily and weekly rest periods
are granted in line with the law that applies at the place of work.

## 7. Pay

Gross pay is [AMOUNT AND CURRENCY] per [MONTH / HOUR / YEAR], paid on or before
[PAY DATE] by bank transfer to an account in the Employee's name.

Where they apply to the position, the following may be paid in addition, under
the terms of the relevant Company policy in force at the time:

- Performance or quality bonus: [DESCRIPTION OR "not applicable"]
- Language allowance: [DESCRIPTION OR "not applicable"]
- Shift, night or weekend premium: [DESCRIPTION OR "not applicable"]
- Other allowances: [DESCRIPTION OR "not applicable"]

Statutory deductions - taxes and social contributions - are withheld as required
by law.

## 8. Overtime

Overtime is worked only when requested or approved in advance by the Employee's
manager. Approved overtime is compensated as [PAID AT X RATE / TIME OFF IN
LIEU], in line with the law that applies at the place of work.

## 9. Paid leave and public holidays

The Employee is entitled to [NUMBER] days of paid annual leave per full calendar
year, in addition to the public holidays observed at the place of work.

Leave is taken by agreement with the Employee's manager, taking into account both
the Employee's wishes and the staffing needs of the service.

## 10. Sickness and other absence

The Employee must inform their manager as early as possible, and no later than
[NOTIFICATION DEADLINE], on any day they cannot attend work, and must provide a
medical certificate where the law or Company policy requires one.

Sick pay, parental leave, and any other special leave are granted in line with
the law that applies at the place of work and the Company policies in force.

## 11. Training

The Company provides an initial training and onboarding programme for this
position. Time spent in required training is working time and is paid.

The Employee agrees to take part in the training reasonably required to perform
the role and to keep required certifications current.

## 12. Confidentiality

Customer data and Company business information are confidential. During the
employment and after it ends, the Employee agrees:

- not to disclose confidential information to anyone outside the Company;
- not to use it for any purpose other than performing the role;
- not to copy, photograph, record or remove customer data from Company systems;
- to follow the Company's information-security rules and access controls.

This clause does not prevent the Employee from making a protected disclosure or
reporting wrongdoing to a competent authority where the law allows it.

## 13. Data protection and monitoring

The Company processes the Employee's personal data for employment purposes, on
the legal bases and for the retention periods set out in the employee privacy
notice provided with the contract.

Calls, screens and messaging in customer-facing roles may be recorded or
monitored for quality, training, security and compliance purposes. Where this
applies to the position, the Company will explain in writing before the start
date what is monitored, why, for how long recordings are kept and who can access
them. Monitoring is limited to what is necessary for those purposes.

## 14. Company property and equipment

Equipment, systems, accounts and access credentials provided by the Company
remain the property of the Company. They are to be used for work, kept secure,
and returned in good condition, together with any Company data, when the
employment ends.

## 15. Work created during employment

Work created by the Employee in the course of their duties belongs to the
Company, to the extent permitted by the law that applies at the place of work.

## 16. Health, safety and respect at work

The Company provides a safe workplace and the equipment and instruction needed
to work safely. The Employee agrees to follow health and safety rules and to
report hazards, incidents and injuries.

The Company does not tolerate harassment, discrimination or violence of any kind.
Concerns can be raised with a manager, with HR, or through the Company's
reporting channel, and raising a concern in good faith will not be held against
the Employee.

## 17. Company policies

Company policies - including the code of conduct, the information-security
policy, the attendance policy and the privacy notice - apply to the employment
and are made available to the Employee. Policies may be updated from time to
time; changes are communicated in advance. Company policies do not form part of
the contract unless the contract says so, and they do not reduce the Employee's
statutory rights.

## 18. Notice and termination

After the probationary period, either party may end the contract by giving
[NOTICE PERIOD] notice in writing, or the statutory minimum notice if that is
longer.

The Company may end the contract without notice only in the cases allowed by the
law that applies at the place of work, and following any procedure that law
requires.

On the last working day the Employee returns all Company property and receives
their final pay, payment for any accrued unused leave, and the employment
documents required by law.

## 19. Changes to this contract

Any change to this contract must be agreed in writing and signed by both parties,
except where a change is required by law or by a collective agreement.

## 20. Governing law and disputes

This contract is governed by the law of [COUNTRY / JURISDICTION OF EMPLOYMENT],
and any collective agreement that applies at the place of work.

If a dispute arises, both parties will first try to resolve it directly and in
good faith. Nothing in this clause prevents either party from bringing a claim
before the competent court or tribunal.

Where any clause of this contract conflicts with a mandatory rule of the law that
applies at the place of work, that rule prevails and the rest of the contract
remains in force.

## 21. Full agreement

Together with the offer letter and the documents attached to it, this contract
sets out the whole agreement between the parties regarding the employment, and
replaces any earlier discussion or correspondence about it.

## 22. Signatures

The real contract is signed by both parties, each keeping a copy.

- For the Company: [NAME], [POSITION]. Date: [DATE]. Signature: ______________________
- The Employee: [FULL NAME]. Date: [DATE]. Signature: ______________________
